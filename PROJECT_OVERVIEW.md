# MedSignal-NoShow — Appointment No-Show Research Prototype

A synthetic appointment-level classification exercise using lead time, prior no-shows, reminders, chronic-condition follow-up, distance, age, weekday, and first-visit status.

Synthetic data only. Research/education only. No patient records are used. A reminder or care decision must remain under qualified human review. Age-band recall and prediction-rate gaps are descriptive diagnostics, not proof of fairness.

Run: `python -m medsignal.train --json`; tests: `python -m pytest -q`.
